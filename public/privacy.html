BEYOND privacy

BEYOND is a personal app made by Gavin Lohnes for his own use.

Your data stays on your device

Everything you record in BEYOND is stored in your browser on your own device. BEYOND has no server and no account system of its own.

Google Drive backup is optional and encrypted

  • Nothing is sent to Google Drive unless you turn Drive backup on.
  • Each backup is encrypted on your device before it is uploaded. Google stores only the encrypted file and cannot read it.
  • Your passphrase and recovery code never leave your device and are never uploaded.
  • BEYOND asks Google only for the drive.file permission, which lets it see and manage only the files BEYOND itself creates in your Drive — nothing else.

What BEYOND does not do

  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • No advertising.

Other services you choose to use

If you connect optional features — such as Google Calendar sync, the Alfred assistant (which sends the request you make to Anthropic using your own key) or IFTTT webhooks — data you choose is sent to that service only when you use it.

How to disconnect

In BEYOND, open MORE → SYSTEMS and choose to disconnect Google Drive. This revokes BEYOND's access; your data stays on your device. You can also remove BEYOND's access at any time from your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps with account access.
