# BEYOND after 3.0 — from daily driver to operational intelligence

Owner direction, 2026-11-03: “Let's create a plan beyond 3.0 launch. Advance it even further.”

This is the product sequence after the 3.0 phone launch, not standing implementation authority for
every item. Each build objective still receives its own bounded approval, brief, risk classification,
verification and merge decision. Field evidence may reorder the sequence.

## Destination

BEYOND 4.0 should not be a larger dashboard or a generic health coach. It should be a personal
operating system that understands Gavin's rotation, commitments, training and recorded reality well
enough to prepare the right context, preserve his choices, and then recede.

The relationship remains:

> **BEYOND knows and recommends. Alfred explains and drafts. Gavin decides.**

Success is not more engagement. Success is fewer repeated decisions, less reconstruction after an
interruption, fewer outside apps, and more inspectable truth.

Master already has more than the original launch checklist recorded:

app, workaround, hesitation and stale assumption recorded.

## Sequence

### Gate 0 — 3.0 in the field

Use BEYOND as the phone daily driver for one complete rotation. Inspect three mandatory moments:
the end of the final shift, the next wake, and a full day off.

Capture only actionable evidence:

- **INCIDENT:** something was wrong, lost, duplicated or unsafe;
- **FRICTION:** the right action took avoidable attention;
- **MISSING JOB:** another app or workaround was required;
- **TRUST GAP:** BEYOND's meaning, source or authority was unclear.

Finish when the rotation has a concise evidence ledger and no unresolved data-loss or core-loop
incident. Incidents and recurring friction outrank the roadmap below.

### 3.1 — The self-running week

Goal: a week is ready without rebuilding it in Gavin's head.

- TRAIN always carries the saved rotation programme into the next session and clearly shows what
  changed, what did not, and why.
- The deterministic local review writes a useful free weekly summary; Alfred may add interpretation
  when enabled, but the history does not depend on a paid call.
- The week view joins shifts, chosen training, planned commitments and saved food preparation without
  claiming unknown time is free.
- At the end of the week, BEYOND compares planned versus recorded reality without adherence scores,
  guilt or silently rescheduling anything.

Finish when the next week can be understood and adjusted from one place, offline, with every saved
change explicitly confirmed.

### 3.2 — Rotation intelligence

Goal: BEYOND understands the transition, not merely the calendar.

- The first sleep after a run of shifts receives an optional protection plan based on Gavin's own
  recorded pattern and current commitments.
- Flip help compares the latest transition with prior recorded flips, carries sample size and
  uncertainty, and offers a draft—not a medical circadian prescription.
- Days-off planning distinguishes recovery, commitments, training and genuinely open time.
- A “since the last shift” factual summary restores context after sleep without creating a score.

Finish when work run → first sleep → first day off → return to nights is one coherent, inspectable
journey. Engine-rule changes, if evidence supports any, remain separately Protected.

### 3.5 — The learning coach

Goal: coaching becomes a continuous, correctable loop rather than a series of chats.

- ALFRED COACH can revisit a saved draft, compare it with recorded reality, and propose a revision.
- Personal experiments name the hypothesis, measure, duration and stop condition before they begin.
- Every interpretation links to records; low evidence reduces confidence and interruption.
- Useful preferences become inspectable memories with correction history, never hidden personality
  scores.

Finish when a plan can survive interruption, be revised without losing its origin, and later answer
“did this help me?” honestly. Alfred still cannot replace or rank Engine recommendations.

### 4.0 — Connected body, owned data

Goal: broaden what BEYOND knows without surrendering ownership or local operation.

- Generalize the existing Health Connect bridge beyond the Garmin-labelled path where the source and
  permissions genuinely support it: compatible watches, scales and selected measurements.
- Preserve source package, measurement time, import time, corrections, missing periods and owner-wins
  rules.
- Add a compact connection and freshness inspector instead of another dashboard.
- Keep logging, TODAY, TRAIN, review and backups useful without an account or signal.

This phase is Protected. Every new data type/provider requires a privacy, permissions, semantics and
maintenance decision. Medical records, glucose interpretation and provider accounts are not implied.

Finish when a second approved source can enter, be inspected, be withdrawn, and survive backup/
restore without changing Engine authority or corrupting provenance.

### 4.5 — Batcomputer

Goal: give deep work a home without turning the phone into a desktop dashboard.

- Desktop COMMAND provides long-range review, programme construction, evidence comparison, backup
  inspection and deliberate planning.
- Phone FIELD remains glance → act → resume life.
- A private share surface exposes only explicitly selected calendar/plan facts and can be revoked.
- Import tools may learn from Hevy/Strong/open formats only after licence, provenance and duplicate
  handling are settled.

Finish when the same truth supports two deliberately different interfaces: FIELD for reality in
motion and COMMAND for careful inspection.

## Order and gates

1. Run the 3.0 field rotation.
2. Repair any incident or repeated friction.
3. Build 3.1 as the next product milestone.
4. Let recorded rotation evidence shape 3.2.
5. Advance coaching only after plans and reviews have durable local truth.
6. Add connected sources before Batcomputer only when they improve an approved phone job.

Do not start several phases at once. A phase is not complete because its screens exist; it is
complete when its whole journey works on the phone, its truth is inspectable, and field use no
longer requires reconstructing the same decision elsewhere.

## Reuse boundary

- **REUSE:** current BEYOND commands, programme, lived-day/rotation projections, weekly review,
  Health Connect bridge, Alfred tools, proposal cards, provenance catalog and suit grammar; proven
  co-created-plan, progressive-disclosure and field/command patterns.
- **OWN:** lived-day semantics, rotation transitions, owner-wins health reconciliation, Engine
  authority, plan-versus-reality history and local-first behavior.
- **BUILD FROM SCRATCH:** none by default. New infrastructure requires evidence that the existing
  platform or a maintained legally reusable primitive cannot serve the job.
