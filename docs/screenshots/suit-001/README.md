`vite preview` on port 5176), with synthetic records, America/Chicago time and reduced motion.

four groups, no writes and focus returned to the line; the offline signal in words on TODAY;
stealth by long-press; blind water logged exactly once with no total shown; a peek that shows
only while held; stealth persisting across an offline cold reload under the service worker; and
Escape exiting with the offline signal still in words.

Browser suites cover the rest: `SuitSystems`, `SuitSignals` (all four signals, glitch-once,
no replay, reduced motion) and `SuitStealth` (keyboard/COMMAND path, focus trap, Escape,
blind meal, undo, save failure, workout continuity, axe at three widths).
