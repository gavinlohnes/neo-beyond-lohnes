# What's new in BEYOND — night of 2026-11-02

Everything below is free unless it says Alfred. Nothing saves until you tap SAVE (or say "save").

## Talk or type anything
- **Search icon = one box.** Type "16 oz water", "protein left?", "week ahead" or "bench".
  It logs (LOG FREE), answers from your records, or jumps to the screen. ASK ALFRED only if you tap it.
- **Unknown food is free.** "Ate 2 slices of pepperoni pizza" → FIND "pepperoni pizza" FREE opens MEAL's
  search. ASK ALFRED TO ESTIMATE is there if you want it.
- **SAVE + REMEMBER MEAL** keeps any meal card as a saved meal, so next time it logs free.
- **Gym sets:** during a workout, "chest press 135 x 8".
- **Fix mistakes:** UNDO on a saved card; or say "that was 12 oz, not 16", "sleep was 7 hours not 6".
- **"When's my next day off?"**, "how much protein do I have left?" answered free.

## Drive home, hands-free
- After a shift, TODAY shows **DRIVE HOME · HANDS-FREE**. Tap it, say what to log, BEYOND reads it back,
  say "save". "That's all" closes it. (Also: search "drive home".)

## Every screen knows where you are
- Under each title: **ON SHIFT · scheduled to 06:00 · scheduled: night 2 of 3 · next day off Thu · Next: …**
- TODAY shows the same rotation line under its mode.
- With no Alfred key, TODAY's closed **BRIEF · Free · from your records** sums up today.

## Calendar (built by Codex)
- Week Ahead → **EXPORT TO CALENDAR**: shifts only (shareable) or shifts + plans, 4 or 8 weeks, as a file
  for Google Calendar.
