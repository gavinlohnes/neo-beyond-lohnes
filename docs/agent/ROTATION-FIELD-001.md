## Run boundary

Start on the next first work night in a run. Finish after the first complete day off following that
run. Use the normal installed app and real records. Do not create synthetic entries to make a screen
look complete.

Three checkpoints are mandatory:

1. **FINAL SHIFT ENDS** — can the shift be closed, handed off and left without reconstruction?
2. **NEXT WAKE** — does BEYOND restore the situation and protect the next decision with little input?
3. **FULL DAY OFF** — can training, food, commitments, recovery and planning live together without
   another app or an improvised workaround?

## Capture in BEYOND

Use **LOG → NOTE** at the moment the evidence occurs. Start the note with exactly one prefix:

- `FIELD · INCIDENT ·` wrong, lost, duplicated, unsafe, blocked or materially stale;
- `FIELD · FRICTION ·` the correct path demanded avoidable attention or reconstruction;
- `FIELD · MISSING JOB ·` another app, paper note or workaround was required;
- `FIELD · TRUST GAP ·` the value, source, uncertainty or authority was unclear.

Write one observable sentence: what happened, where, and what you expected. A screenshot is useful
only when the visual state matters. Do not turn preferences or speculative ideas into incidents.
If nothing failed at a mandatory checkpoint, capture `FIELD · CLEAR · <checkpoint>` so absence is
recorded rather than inferred.

- Stop and report immediately for data loss, corruption, unsafe recommendation behavior, broken

- Existing workarounds stay visible in the evidence; do not silently compensate for BEYOND.
- Field notes are ordinary owner-controlled notes. Nothing is uploaded or automatically converted
