# GRAYSON-DAY-TARGETS — a calorie target for each kind of day (owner go, 2026-11-25)

Gavin's merge of the [brief](../agent/GRAYSON-DAY-TARGETS-001.md) approves these; #736 records them.

- **Off until he turns it on.** Off, every reader shows exactly the plain target with the same words as before.
  Turning it on shows four adjustments at 0, so nothing is guessed for him.
- **Kinds come from his rota and training plan, not weekdays,** because Week A and Week B don't follow the calendar:
  Work night (WORK_RUN), First day off (RECOVERY), Day off (FREE and FLIP), named the same way the eating window names
  them. A flip counts as a day off: he stays on night hours and plans a flip as an exception. A day the schedule can't
  place uses the plain target.
- **Lift day** adds on top of the kind: a strength workout his programme assigns to that day, or one logged that day. A
  run alone isn't one; a sequence programme (A → B → C) assigns no day, so it marks none.
- **His numbers:** signed adjustments in steps of 50 within ±1000, each with UNDO. His floor holds and the day says
  "held at your floor". Protein stays one daily number.
- **The week's total is held by a line and one tap, not by the app moving numbers:** "Over two weeks: … your target"
  (within half a step), and BALANCE sets the day-off adjustment, with UNDO. Nothing balances itself.
- **The weekly proposal is unchanged:** saving it moves the base; the adjustments ride on top.
