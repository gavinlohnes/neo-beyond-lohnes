# GRAYSON-BLOCK-CYCLE — 4 to 6 week blocks, a lighter last week, and a review he decides by tap (owner go, 2026-11-25)

Gavin approved the [brief](../agent/GRAYSON-BLOCK-CYCLE-001.md) ("do these work outs for 4 to 6 weeks ... then Delta
and I will review"; "Brief all of it"). It amends TRAIN Intelligence §7 ("no automatic calendar deload"): a lighter
last week is planned only inside a block he starts, shown before it begins and turned off in one tap.

- **A block** is one `TRAINING_BLOCK_SET` event (global, USER, latest wins) with UNDO: the coming Monday, 4 to 6 weeks
  (5 by default), 0 to 3 focus areas, lighter last week on by default. He can change it or end it early.
- **The light week** keeps the same exercises and weights with about a third fewer working sets (rounded down, never
  below 1). It changes the day's prescription only, never the saved routine. NORMAL THIS WEEK turns it off. Its
  sessions never count toward progression's history, stalls or the weekly stall finding.
- **The review** (from the block's last day, offered as one Delta card) shows at most five lines with their numbers:
  sessions done of planned (planned from the week plan), main lifts' estimated 1RM start to end, stalls
  (deloadStall's four sessions without a record), trend weight (only with weigh-ins in the first and last week), and
  focus sets a week.
- **Suggestions, at most three, from fixed rules, ranked:** a stall → swap to a same-muscle move (WORKOUT-CHANGES'
  ranker), or, with none, start it lighter next block; sessions under the share of planned → make the most-skipped day
  a rest day; every main lift rising and on plan → keep the routines; a focus area under its range → one more set on
  its first exercise. ACCEPT saves through ROUTINE-EDIT or WEEK-PLAN (or the block, for a lighter start) with UNDO;
  SKIP and no answer change nothing. NEXT BLOCK starts the next with the same settings.
- **Evidence floor (§7):** no suggestion rests on one session: stalls need four, sessions are judged over the block.
- **A lighter start** pre-fills the lift lighter (rounded down to its weight step) until he logs it in the block; it's
  an editable display default, never a change to progression.
- **Every threshold is his** (Block settings, with RESET TO DEFAULTS): the sessions share (75%), the weight drop (10%),
  the light week's share (a third), and the focus ranges (§7's chest 8–10, triceps 4–6, traps 6–8 a week; others his).
