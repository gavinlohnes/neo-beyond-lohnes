# GRAYSON Training Intelligence

Status: **OWNER-AUTHORIZED DESIGN CANDIDATE, NOT IMPLEMENTATION** - objective
`GRAYSON-TRAIN-INTELLIGENCE-001`, 2026-11-20. This refines the merged
[Integrated Health Mission](GRAYSON_INTEGRATED_HEALTH_MISSION.md) and
[Guided Operating Loop](GRAYSON_GUIDED_OPERATING_LOOP.md). It authorizes no product code,
navigation, Engine, command/event, schema, provider, dependency, demonstration media, Google Drive
change or implementation.

## 1. Product judgment

TRAIN should replace the jobs Gavin would otherwise need a workout logger, exercise reference and
coach to perform, without inheriting social feeds, leaderboards, public comparison, streak pressure
or noisy encouragement.

> **Training intelligence principle:** show today's useful session first; make execution faster than
> analysis; teach at the moment of need; preserve the balanced programme beneath temporary emphasis;
> and change the smallest thing supported by evidence. Delta proposes and explains. Gavin decides.

The complete loop is:

> **Mission -> programme -> today's workout -> recorded performance -> progress assessment ->
> owner-approved adaptation.**

Exercise demonstrations are deliberately deferred. Setup text, two or three cues, common mistakes,
equipment alternatives and a personal cue are enough for the first useful system. Future licensed
or original media needs its own objective, provenance and maintenance plan.

## 3. Programme identity

### Sequence, not weekdays

The default is an ordered **A/B/C full-body rotation**, not a Monday/Wednesday/Friday contract.
Work, sleep and life may move the opportunities without breaking the programme. Missing one day does
not create catch-up debt or force adjacent sessions.

### Approved starting architecture

| Session | Primary job | Balanced work | Performance support |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A - bench strength** | Barbell bench press; triceps | Leg press; seated cable row | sit-up practice; hamstring/lower-back mobility |
| **B - pull and posterior chain** | Lat pulldown; traps/upper back | dumbbell RDL or leg curl; shoulder press; incline press | plank or dead bug |
| **C - bench volume and balance** | Lighter/higher-repetition bench; chest/triceps | lunge/squat or leg press; row/pulldown; calves | one-minute sit-up development |

Exact exercises remain owner-selectable and equipment-confirmed. Planet Fitness is the normal
context, and Gavin confirmed access to a true barbell bench station. Free-weight bench may therefore
be trained and measured directly; Smith, machine and dumbbell pressing remain useful supporting or
fallback movements, not equivalent evidence for a free-weight test.

### Four legitimate forms

1. **Full:** approximately 45-65 minutes.
2. **Short:** essential movements, approximately 25-35 minutes.
3. **Minimum:** two or three useful movements, approximately 10-20 minutes.
4. **Home:** bodyweight squat/lunge, push-up level, bridge, core and mobility; honestly missing loaded
   pull work when no suitable equipment exists.

These are design destinations. They do not amend current STANDARD/REDUCED/RECOVERY semantics.

## 4. Exercise intelligence

GRAYSON begins with a curated personal core, not a catalogue Gavin must organize. Preserve the ten
built-in movements first, then add only movements that serve balance, emphasis, home fallback or
the four performance standards.

### Exercise-card contract

Each proposed reference can contain:

- stable exercise identity and movement family;
- primary and secondary muscles as descriptive anatomy, not a growth score;
- equipment, location and machine label where comparability matters;
- programme role and why it is present;
- setup instructions, two or three cues and common mistakes;
- easier, harder, home and equipment alternatives;
- owner-confirmed personal cue;
- applicable rep range and existing history/progress;
- `USE IN PROGRAMME` / `REPLACE CURRENT EXERCISE` only in a later authorized write journey.

The initial core should cover current A/B/C movements; true/dumbbell/incline bench; push-up levels;
cable fly; assisted dip/pull-up; overhead triceps extension; face pull, shrug and lateral raise;
one-arm row; leg extension, RDL, lunge, calf raise, hip abduction and bridge; hammer curl; squat,
plank and dead bug; and test-specific sit-up and sit-and-reach practice. Couch-to-5K and later
1.5-mile work remain conditioning tracks, not fake muscle-library cards.

Third-party databases may inform field shape and taxonomy. Proprietary instructions, illustrations
and videos are not copied. The prior free-exercise-db ruling remains a shape reference, not authority
for bulk import.

## 5. Guided setup and first calibration

Delta starts from the Mission, existing history, confirmed equipment and schedule. It does not ask
Gavin to design a split, estimate a one-repetition maximum or understand programming terminology.

### First-session phone journey

1. **TRAIN home:** `SESSION A · BENCH STRENGTH`, purpose, estimated duration and
   `BEGIN CALIBRATION`; alternatives are SHORTEN, HOME and NOT TRAINING TODAY.
2. **Briefing:** calibration is not evaluation or a maximum test. Ask only current pain/restriction,
   perceived capacity and available time when unknown.
3. **Preparation:** show purpose, setup, three cues, common mistakes and available alternative.
4. **Ramp:** begin with an empty bar/light load, collect simple difficulty feedback, propose a
   conservative next preparation set, then ask approval for the first working load.
5. **Work:** fast weight/repetition entry, LOG SET, exact repeat and rest. Effort, technique
   uncertainty, discomfort and personal cue remain optional.
6. **Adjust:** briefly propose a smaller/larger next load only when recorded feedback justifies it.
7. **Complete:** summarize facts; propose starting loads, equipment labels, personal cues and session
   practicality as separate confirm/change/not-yet understandings.
8. **Learn:** B and C calibrate in turn. A starting programme becomes provisional only after relevant
   exposures, with confidence and missing evidence visible.

Delta never says it “saw” technique without an authorized observation source. A heavy 1RM attempt is
not required to establish a working load. Pain pauses the movement and offers stop/alternative/end;
Delta does not diagnose.

## 6. Delta during training

### Delta Training Intervention Rule

> **During training, Delta remains quiet when execution is proceeding normally. It intervenes
> briefly when recorded evidence justifies an adjustment, immediately when Gavin reports pain or a
> safety concern, and provides detailed reasoning on request. Proposed learnings are consolidated
> after the workout for Gavin to inspect, confirm, correct or postpone.**

| Situation | Delta behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Normal set | Log, start rest and stay quiet |
| Meaningful recorded change | One concise proposal with accept/modify/decline |
| Pain or safety report | Pause affected movement immediately; offer safe next actions, no diagnosis |
| `WHY?` | Evidence used, uncertainty, alternatives and consequence |
| Finish | One consolidated learning review, never hidden retention |

Restraint is part of intelligence. Delta does not manufacture commentary to appear active.

## 7. Programme adaptation

### Smallest justified change

Use this ladder:

1. improve technique and repeatability;
2. add repetitions within the existing prescription;
3. add the smallest practical load;
4. add a set only when more recoverable dosage is justified;
5. substitute an exercise while preserving its role;
6. redesign the programme only when broader evidence shows the structure is failing.

Existing Engine progression remains authoritative until explicitly amended. A later implementation
may present double-progression context around it, but cannot silently reinterpret recorded effort or
change the load.

### Evidence floors

- One poor exposure ordinarily causes **HOLD**, not a programme rewrite.
- A stall requires comparable exercise/equipment and prescription across multiple exposures, with
  missing sessions, interruption, pain and unusual recovery context separated.
- A cross-domain association is not a cause. “Short sleep accompanied two lower sessions” is
  permitted; “sleep caused the stall” is not established.
- Missing logs are unknown, never proof that food, sleep or training did not occur.
- Estimated 1RM is labelled estimated; an observed test remains a separate record.

### Adaptation outcomes

- **Hold:** repeat the prescription and gather another comparable exposure.
- **Progress:** add repetitions or the smallest practical load after the current range is completed
  with acceptable technique/effort evidence.
- **Reduce today:** preserve primary movements while removing later accessory burden.
- **Recovery:** light movement or rest when the current evidence and explicit condition justify it;
  no automatic calendar deload.
- **Substitute:** pain/safety, repeated equipment absence, strong owner preference, persistent
  technique barrier or movement-specific failure; preserve programme role and keep histories honest.
- **Review programme:** only after repeated evidence shows a broader mismatch.

### Training Emphasis Rule

> **GRAYSON uses four-to-six-week emphasis blocks to develop selected muscle groups while preserving
> a balanced full-body programme. Weekly adjustments may reduce, move or temporarily substitute work
> in response to schedule, recovery, discomfort or changing circumstances. These adjustments change
> dosage, not the programme's underlying identity. A new emphasis begins only through Gavin's
> informed choice.**

Gavin selects zero to three focus areas. Delta states the additional dosage and what remains balanced.
The starting chest/triceps/traps design should remain conservative: roughly 8-10 direct chest sets,
4-6 direct triceps sets plus pressing and 6-8 relevant trap/upper-back sets per complete rotation,
then adjust from actual tolerance and outcomes. These are programme starting ranges, not personalized
scientific truths.

At block end, continue, change focus or return to balanced development. Lack of progress triggers
investigation, not automatic volume escalation.

## 8. TRAIN Progress and Direction

TRAIN still opens on today's workout. Programme progress and the muscle map remain one tap away.
The Progress opening answers: **Where am I? Where am I going? Is this working? What should change?**

### Phone order

1. Delta's one-paragraph verdict and confidence.
2. **Current mission:** phased weight direction, strength/muscle objective, rotation and four
   performance standards.
3. **This emphasis block:** focus, intended/completed dosage, rotations remaining and any honest gap.
4. **Performance trajectories:** bodyweight-relative free-weight bench, one-minute sit-up,
   sit-and-reach and 1.5-mile run, each with protocol/source and last observed result.
5. **Strength and muscle coverage:** comparable exercise direction and planned/completed training
   exposure.
6. **Consistency and recovery context:** planned versus completed/shortened/missed with reason when
   known; no streak or guilt.
7. Detailed evidence and proposed adaptation on demand.

### Muscle map boundary

The front/back map is a coverage and navigation instrument. It may show selected focus, planned and
completed dosage, related exercises, reported discomfort and missing balance. It must not shade a
muscle as “grown,” infer hypertrophy from sets or imply that anatomy is measured.

### Evidence vocabulary

| Class | Permitted example | Not permitted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Observed** | “90 lb × 8 was recorded on this bench” | “Your form was good” without observation |
| **Derived estimate** | “Estimated 1RM: 114 lb” with method and inputs | treating estimate as a passed test |
| **Proxy** | “9 of 10 planned chest sets completed” | “Chest grew” |
| **Association** | “Weight declined while three lifts improved” | “The deficit built muscle” |
| **Unknown** | “Running evidence is too limited to judge” | zero or failure |

### Delta assessment states

- **WORKING:** supported direction; preserve the plan.
- **UNCERTAIN:** promising or conflicting evidence below the floor; gather more.
- **NEEDS ATTENTION:** a real gap that merits inspection, not automatic change.
- **READY TO ADJUST:** enough evidence for one explicit owner-controlled proposal.

Every verdict exposes `WHY?`: evidence, missing information, plausible alternatives, confidence,
proposed action and consequence of declining. Accept, modify, not now and keep unchanged remain valid.

## 9. Connected health boundaries

- Bodyweight decline plus maintained/improving lifts is encouraging but does not prove muscle gain.
- Optional waist/circumference, owner-controlled photos or consistent body-composition readings could
  add evidence later; none is required and device limitations remain visible.
- Protein, food, hydration, sleep and work context may explain feasibility or support a hypothesis.
  They do not automatically cause programme changes.
- Medication and symptoms remain private, clinician-led context. TRAIN may respond to an explicit
  symptom/safety statement but never manages treatment.
- Couch-to-5K remains two required plus one optional opportunity at first; later 1.5-mile specificity
  is a distinct phase coordinated with strength and recovery.

## 11. Ranked implementation sequence

1. **TRAIN exercise understanding and calibration preview.** Read existing exercise/history data;
   show the selected exercise's role, setup/cues, alternatives and a no-write calibration walkthrough
   for Session A. This validates teaching and evidence language without activating a new programme.
2. **Mission programme draft.** Evolve the earlier `GRAYSON-TRAIN-PLAN-DRAFT-001`: preview the full
   A/B/C, emphasis dosage and all four variants from existing records, stopping before writes.
3. **Explicit programme activation.** Separately adjudicate template creation/assignment, custom
   rotation selection, commands/events and backup/schema implications.
4. **Fast guided execution.** Add approved intervention grammar around existing live-workout
   recording without changing set semantics.
5. **Progress and Direction.** Compose existing strength, bodyweight, performance and adherence
   evidence with floors and `WHY?`.
6. **Owner-approved adaptation.** Only after field evidence, authorize programme-level proposals,
   block changes and connected Mission review.
7. **Later:** original/licensed demonstrations, richer measurements or watch integration only when
   separately valuable and approved.

## 13. Evidence and donor disposition

- ACSM's updated overview supports regular resistance training, heavier loading specificity for
  strength, multiple sets and progressive stimulus while finding no need for needless complexity:
  <https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000003897>.
- Pelland et al. find volume and frequency have dose-response relationships with diminishing returns;
  this supports cautious dosage, not a personalized optimum:
  <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41343037/>.
- Refalo et al. support proximity-to-failure as a continuum and do not justify mandatory failure:
  <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38970765/>.
- Hevy donates fast set entry, previous-performance context and layered history. Reject its social
  feed, leaderboard/public comparison and streak pressure for this Life OS.
- MuscleWiki donates anatomical discovery and exercise alternatives. Use a body map for coverage and
  navigation, not copied media or fabricated physique inference.
- Planet Fitness donates equipment categories and first-party machine instruction/club support;
  equipment remains owner-confirmed and proprietary media is linked rather than copied.
- NHS Couch to 5K remains the shift-adapted running foundation already adjudicated by the Integrated
  Health Mission.

Commercial patterns are ideas only. No external product supplies authority, copied assets or a
replacement template. This document follows the GRAYSON Evolution Rule: BEYOND remains the working
foundation.
