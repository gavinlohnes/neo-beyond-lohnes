A · COCKPIT LAYOUT

How a pilot reads a whole aircraft in a second. Fixed positions, scales instead of bare numbers, and the screen goes quiet when everything is normal.

A1S

Dark cockpit

Airbus / Boeing philosophy

When a system is normal, its light is off. Vitals in range fade to almost nothing; only what is off-normal lights up. A quiet screen means you're fine.

Fits · nothing hidden, one tap shows all

A2M

Primary display with tapes

Primary Flight Display

Vertical tapes on each side: sleep on the left, water or fuel on the right, scrolling past your usual band like airspeed and altitude. The middle is the situation: night, shift countdown, next step.

Fits · scales, not scores

A3M

Alert stack

EICAS crew alerting

One list, ranked by severity: warning, caution, advisory. Each line in plain words, each opens its detail. Replaces scattered warnings with one place you trust.

Ruling · groups existing Attention and System Status into one list

A4M

Flight plan with waypoints

FMS route · Flighty

Your lived day as a route: nap, eat, shift, breaks, last coffee, sleep. Waypoints come from your schedule and your own usual times. Logging ticks them off.

Fits · built from your schedule and patterns

A5S

Bingo and projected finish

Fighter fuel states · HUD energy

Where you'll finish at this pace ("2.1 L by 16:30"), plus a bingo point: the latest time you can still reach the target at a sane rate. Pace, not judgment.

Fits · arithmetic on your own logs

A6L

Multi-function pages

MFD with soft keys

TODAY becomes a few fixed pages you swipe between: status, route, systems, log. Same place every time, so your thumb learns it.

Ruling · changes TODAY's layout (protected)

B · NIGHT OPERATIONS

You work nights. Cockpits and dive computers solve for reading a screen in the dark without wrecking your eyes or your sleep.

B1S

Night vision mode

Apple Watch Ultra · cockpit night lighting

On shift after dark, the whole screen goes dim red-on-black, the way the Ultra's dial does. It protects your dark-adapted eyes and keeps the screen discreet.

Conflicts · "red only for the next step" is locked. Needs your amendment.

B2S

Stealth by phase

BEYOND's own stealth · cockpit dimming

Stealth already exists behind a long-press. This makes it the default look on shift after midnight: dim, time and quick logs only, everything else one tap away.

Ruling · changes when stealth turns on

B3M

Your circadian map

Rise Science · Timeshifter

When your energy usually dips on a night, from your own check-ins, plus light and caffeine timing that protects tomorrow's sleep. Only from your data, labelled as pattern, not prediction.

Ruling · new guidance on light and caffeine timing

C · DEPTH ON DEMAND

What Oura, WHOOP, Apple and Garmin do best: a calm summary that opens into real analysis. Everything here uses data BEYOND already has.

C1M

The night, drawn

Oura sleep timeline

One sleep as a stepped timeline with wake-ups, if Garmin sends stages. If it doesn't, the same view still shows start, end and interruptions.

Fits · I check what Garmin import stores first

C2S

Trend arrows

Apple Fitness trends

Each system gets an arrow comparing your last 90 days with your last year: up, down or steady, in words. The long view in one glance.

Fits · read-only

C3M

What moves you

WHOOP journal impact

Your experiments and tagged days, compared honestly: what tends to go with better energy or sleep for you, with day counts and "no clear difference" when there isn't one.

Fits · extends TRY; patterns, not causes

C4M

Muscle readiness map

Fitbod recovery map

A plain body outline shaded by days since each muscle group was trained, from your logged sets. Answers "what can I train tonight?" at a glance.

Fits · days since, not a recovery score

C5M

Flight recorder

Black box · Strava replay

Scrub through any past lived day minute by minute and see what you logged, when, and what BEYOND showed you. Good for "what went wrong on Tuesday?"

Fits · uses the event history

C6S

Contributor bars

Oura readiness contributors

Under any vital, the few things that usually go with it for you, each as a bar against your normal. No combined number at the top: you read the parts.

Fits · as long as there's no headline score

Oura · WHOOP · Garmin

D · THE SUIT'S CHARACTER

Personality from how it talks and what it remembers, the way a good crew chief or mentor does. Calm, brief, never cute.

D1M

Preflight checklist

Electronic checklist

Before each shift, a short checklist built from your PREP items and memory. Logging auto-ticks lines. Two minutes, and nothing gets forgotten at 18:40.

Fits · reuses PREP and Codex's memory

D2M

After-action report

Military AAR · flight debrief

The shift debrief, upgraded: planned versus actual from your logs, the gap, and one thing to carry forward. You confirm it in one tap.

Fits · builds on LOOP-002's handoff

D3S

Voice setting

CARROT Weather personality dial

Choose how the suit speaks: silent (numbers only), terse (one line), or mentor (one line plus the why). Same facts either way.

Fits · presentation only

D4M

Run briefing

Mission briefing

Before each block of nights: what usually goes wrong for you in this run, your plan, and the flip back. Built from rotation patterns, lessons and experiments.

Fits · read-only; you write the objective

D5S

Service record

Pilot logbook

A quiet logbook page of what you've done: runs, nights, lessons, trials, clean days. Facts, not badges or streaks.

Fits · counts, never streaks

E · FEEL IN THE HAND

How the controls answer. Codex's SUIT-002 already gave saves a buzz and a pulse; these push the physical side further.

Fits · extends SUIT-002

Glance without opening

Inspect anything

Halide pro mode · instrument tooltips

Press and hold any number anywhere to see where it came from, whether it was corrected, and what it feeds. Provenance is already in BEYOND; this makes it universal.
